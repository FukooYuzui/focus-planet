# 专注星球应用图标

版本：0.7.2。使用 imagegen 内置图像生成工具绘制，未使用 CLI/API fallback。

画面由蓝绿色小星球、幼苗、奶油色计时轨道和星星组成，延续 3D 卡通养成主题。原图保留透明通道。应用图标仅用 WPF 做等比缩放和 PNG/ICO 格式编码，没有额外绘画或修改图案。

## 资产

- `src/FocusGarden.App/Assets/FocusPlanet-source.png`：1254 × 1254 透明原图。
- `src/FocusGarden.App/Assets/FocusPlanet-256.png`：窗口、任务栏和侧栏使用的透明 PNG。
- `src/FocusGarden.App/Assets/FocusPlanet.ico`：16、20、24、32、40、48、64、128、256 像素九种尺寸，嵌入 Windows EXE。

原图留在源码包，运行程序只内嵌 ICO 与 256 像素图片，避免重复携带大图。

## 最终生成提示词

Asset type: a single production Windows desktop application icon for 专注星球 (Focus Planet), a gentle six-month planet-growing Pomodoro focus app. Create a polished 3D cartoon app icon on a genuinely transparent background. Main subject: one large rounded miniature planet with mint-green organic land and turquoise-blue sea, a tiny fresh two-leaf sprout growing from its upper surface, and a warm ivory orbital ring that subtly evokes a circular focus timer. A single small golden star close to the ring is enough to suggest space. Match a calm midnight-blue, mint-green and warm cream identity. Smooth sculpted toy-like shapes, soft bevels, tasteful directional light, clear dimensional volume and clean dark-teal silhouette edging. Composition: centered, square, one icon only, planet fills most of the image, generous safe margins around the ring and sprout; strong readable silhouette down to 32 pixels. Keep the design simple and distinctive, avoid tiny buildings, forests or decorative detail. No lettering, numbers, slogan, watermark, mockup, frame, app window, or icon grid. No rectangular background plate, no cast shadow outside the object, no checkerboard pattern. Output a clean high-resolution transparent square PNG with intact antialiased alpha edges.
